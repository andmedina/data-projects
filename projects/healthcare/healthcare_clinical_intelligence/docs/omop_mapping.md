# OMOP Extension

OMOP is Phase 7, after the native clinical model has trustworthy lineage. Planned mappings include Patient to `PERSON`, Encounter to `VISIT_OCCURRENCE`, Condition to `CONDITION_OCCURRENCE`, Medication to `DRUG_EXPOSURE`, Procedure to `PROCEDURE_OCCURRENCE`, and Observation/labs to `MEASUREMENT`.

Mapping must use approved vocabulary concepts and document standard/non-standard concept decisions. This project will not claim OMOP conformance until a validated ETL and Achilles-style quality checks are implemented.
